An ESPHome build on a Waveshare ESP32-S3 1.9" LCD display board, monitoring brewing and shed conditions — gas pressure, fridge temperature, and ambient temperature/humidity. Integrates with Home Assistant and displays live readings on the device itself.

Work in progress.
