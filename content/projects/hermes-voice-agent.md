Voice access to a personal Hermes agent through two clients: a phone call (SIP into LiveKit, then a Pipecat bot with voice activity detection, local speech-to-text, the Hermes voice profile and text-to-speech), and an e-ink satellite built on ESPHome. Everything runs on one laptop over Tailscale: no public ports, no phone number, no custom phone app.
